📊 **Credit Card Financial Performance Analysis**

🧾 **Overview**  
This project involves the analysis of weekly credit card transaction and customer data to understand revenue trends, customer behavior, and overall financial performance. The findings are presented using a Power BI dashboard exported as a PDF for easy review.

🎯 **Problem Statement**  
Financial teams require regular visibility into credit card usage, revenue contribution, and customer segments to track performance and identify growth or risk patterns. This project addresses that need through structured data analysis and visualization.

📂 **Data Description**  
The analysis is based on:
- Credit card transaction records
- Customer demographic details (age, income, gender, location)
- Weekly time-based financial data  

All relevant data files used for the analysis are included in this repository.

🛠️ **Tools Used**
- **SQL** – data extraction and transformation  
- **Power BI** – data modeling and dashboard development  
- **DAX** – calculated measures and time-based analysis  
- **CSV / Excel** – data preparation and handling  

📈 **Metrics Analyzed**
- Total revenue  
- Transaction volume and value  
- Interest earned  
- Week-over-week revenue change  
- Customer activation rate  
- Delinquency rate  
- Revenue contribution by card type and customer segment  

💡 **Key Business Insights**
- Revenue demonstrates a consistent week-over-week increase driven by higher transaction activity.  
- Male customers contribute a larger portion of total revenue compared to female customers.  
- Blue and Silver credit cards account for the majority of transaction volume.  
- A limited number of states contribute a significant share of total revenue.  
- Activation rate suggests moderate customer engagement with scope for improvement.  
- Delinquency rate remains relatively low, indicating controlled credit risk.  

📄 **Dashboard Output**  
The Power BI dashboard has been exported as a PDF and is available in this repository for review. It provides a consolidated view of key metrics, trends, and customer segmentation.

🔍 **How to Review the Project**
- Open the dashboard PDF to explore trends and insights.  
- Review the dataset files to understand the underlying data used for analysis.
